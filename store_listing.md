### Full description

DEWater Tracker counts water bottles you use. It is an object tracker, not a health or hydration app.

The log starts empty. Nothing is added until you add it.

💧 Today
See how many bottles you logged today. Give each one a name and an emoji, set how many, and add a note if you want. Change the time if you are catching up on earlier bottles.

📅 History
Browse past days. Open a day to review it, then edit or delete any bottle.

📊 Statistics
A daily graph for the week, plus a weekly summary: total bottles, daily average, busiest day, and how many days had a bottle. Move to earlier or later weeks. An all-time total stays on the page.

📋 Copy
A gradient Copy button copies today’s log, your history, or the week you are viewing. Share that text only when you tap Share.

What you control
• Add, edit, and delete every entry
• The app starts empty
• Logs stay on your device
• No account and no sign-in

DEWater Tracker does not give medical advice, hydration goals, or health recommendations. It only keeps the bottle count you enter.

Made to stay small, clear, and easy to use.
